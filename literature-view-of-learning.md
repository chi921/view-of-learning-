# View of Learning — 文献补充清单

> 主题：According to social constructivism, how do people learn? What is the role of the learner, the teacher and the environment?
> 用途：为 pre 补充理论支撑，可用于 slide 4–6 与参考文献页
> 格式：APA 7th
> 说明：以下均为**一手文献**，可直接列入 reference list（前提是你确实读过；严格 APA 只列实际阅读的来源）

---

## 一、总纲：人如何学习

### 1. National Research Council (2000)
National Research Council. (2000). *How people learn: Brain, mind, experience, and school* (Expanded ed.). National Academies Press.

**核心观点**：有效学习环境有四个中心——**学习者中心**（尊重原有概念）、**知识中心**（结构化理解而非碎片）、**评估中心**（评估与教学一体）、**共同体中心**（学习者共同体）。四者恰好对应你要讲的 learner / teacher / environment 三角。

**放在哪**：slide 6 结尾，作为"三个角色其实是一个系统"的理论收束。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "Learning environments should be learner-centred, knowledge-centred, assessment-centred and community-centred — four lenses that map directly onto the learner, the teacher and the environment."

---

### 2. Palincsar, A. S. (1998)
Palincsar, A. S. (1998). Social constructivist perspectives on teaching and learning. *Annual Review of Psychology, 49*, 345–375. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.49.1.345

**核心观点**：社会建构主义的核心不是"学生自己发现"，而是**在他人中介下参与文化实践**。她强调教师的作用是"gradual release"——从示范到共同承担再到学生独立。

**放在哪**：slide 4（学习者）+ slide 5（教师）之间的桥梁，说明"建构"不等于"放任"。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "Social constructivism treats learning as apprenticeship into a community's practices — the teacher's task is the gradual release of responsibility, not the transmission of content (Palincsar, 1998)."

---

## 二、学习者的角色

### 3. Driver, R., Asoko, H., Leach, J., Mortimer, E., & Scott, P. (1994)
Driver, R., Asoko, H., Leach, J., Mortimer, E., & Scott, P. (1994). Constructing scientific knowledge in the classroom. *Educational Researcher, 23*(7), 5–12.

**核心观点**：学生带着**既有概念（prior conceptions）**进入课堂，学习是"个人建构"与"社会建构"的双重过程——既要在头脑中重组概念，也要被引入科学共同体的话语方式。

**放在哪**：slide 4，直接支撑"learner brings prior knowledge"，且天然贴合 STEAM（科学课堂）。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "Learners do not arrive empty: they bring prior conceptions, and conceptual change requires both personal restructuring and induction into a community's ways of knowing (Driver et al., 1994)."

---

## 三、教师的角色

### 4. Wood, D., Bruner, J. S., & Ross, G. (1976)
Wood, D., Bruner, J. S., & Ross, G. (1976). The role of tutoring in problem solving. *Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 17*(2), 89–100. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.1976.tb00381.x

**核心观点**：首次提出 **scaffolding（脚手架）**——专家控制学习者尚不能胜任的部分，让学习者专注在能力边缘的部分，并随能力提升逐步撤除支持。

**放在哪**：slide 5，替代或补充现在只用 Adams 转述的脚手架说法（这是"一手定义"，学术性更强）。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "Scaffolding controls those elements of a task that are initially beyond the learner's capacity, permitting the learner to concentrate on what is within reach (Wood et al., 1976, p. 90)."

---

### 5. Mercer, N., & Howe, C. (2012)
Mercer, N., & Howe, C. (2012). Explaining the dialogic processes of teaching and learning: The value and potential of sociocultural theory. *Learning, Culture and Social Interaction, 1*(1), 12–21. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lcsi.2012.03.001

**核心观点**：课堂对话的质量决定学习质量；教师通过**对话性教学（dialogic teaching）**把学生的日常语言引向学科话语。

**放在哪**：slide 5 或游戏 Scene 1 的反馈，解释"为什么提问比给答案更有效"。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "It is the quality of classroom dialogue, not the quantity of teacher talk, that predicts learning (Mercer & Howe, 2012)."

---

## 四、环境的角色

### 6. Black, P., & Wiliam, D. (1998)
Black, P., & Wiliam, D. (1998). Assessment and classroom learning. *Assessment in Education, 5*(1), 7–74. https://doi.org/10.1080/0969595980050102

**核心观点**：**形成性评估（assessment for learning）**——反馈、自我评估、同伴评估能显著提升学习；评估应嵌在学习过程中。

**放在哪**：slide 6，作为 Adams 转引 Shepard 的"一手升级版"（若你读过 Black & Wiliam，就不必再用二次引用）。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "Formative assessment — feedback, peer assessment and self-assessment — produces some of the largest gains in learning of any classroom intervention (Black & Wiliam, 1998)."

---

## 五、学术争议（硕士层次加分项）

### 7. Kirschner, P. A., Sweller, J., & Clark, R. E. (2006)
Kirschner, P. A., Sweller, J., & Clark, R. E. (2006). Why minimal guidance during instruction does not work: An analysis of the failure of constructivist, discovery, problem-based, experiential, and inquiry-based teaching. *Educational Psychologist, 41*(2), 75–86. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep4102_1

**核心观点**：基于认知负荷理论，**纯发现式/最小指导**对新手无效；建构主义需要**强指导**配合。

**放在哪**：slide 6 的"开放辩论"位置——说明社会建构主义 ≠ 放任探究，STEAM 项目必须配脚手架。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "Constructivism is often misread as discovery learning; cognitive-load research shows novices need strong guidance, which is precisely what scaffolding provides (Kirschner et al., 2006)."

---

### 8. Hmelo-Silver, C. E., Duncan, R. G., & Chinn, C. A. (2007)
Hmelo-Silver, C. E., Duncan, R. G., & Chinn, C. A. (2007). Scaffolding and achievement in problem-based and inquiry learning: A response to Kirschner, Sweller, and Clark (2006). *Educational Psychologist, 42*(2), 99–107. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep4202_1

**核心观点**：对 Kirschner 等的回应——**问题式/探究式学习本身包含大量脚手架**，批评者把"有指导的探究"误当成"无指导的发现"。

**放在哪**：紧接第 7 条，形成一正一反的学术对话，展示批判性阅读。

**可直接用的英文句**：
> "PBL and inquiry learning are not minimally guided; they embed scaffolds that make inquiry productive for novices (Hmelo-Silver et al., 2007)."

## 八、使用提醒

1. **二次引用可替换**：如果读了 Black & Wiliam (1998)，slide 6 就可以把 "Shepard (2000, as cited in Adams, 2006)" 换成 "(Black & Wiliam, 1998)"，Reference List 也不用再依赖转引说明。
2. **不要为凑数而列**：APA 7 要求 reference list 与正文一一对应，正文没引用的条目不能出现。
3. **页码**：直接引语必须带页码；以上"可直接用的英文句"里多数是转述（paraphrase），无需页码，只有 Wood et al. 那句是直接引用，已标 p. 90。
4. **建议优先级**：时间有限时先加 **Driver et al. (1994)**（STEAM 贴合度最高）与 **Wood et al. (1976)**（脚手架一手定义），这两条性价比最高。
