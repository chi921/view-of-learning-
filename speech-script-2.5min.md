## 0:00 – 0:12 ｜ Slide 1 Cover

Good morning. One question: according to social constructivism, **how do people learn** — and what do the learner, the teacher and the environment each do? I'll show you **both sides** of one classroom.

---

## 0:12 – 0:35 ｜ Slide 2 Core Idea

Knowledge isn't poured into a head — it's **built**. People construct meaning by talking, testing and agreeing. Heylighen puts it sharply: what we call truth is what a community accepts through **consensus**. Learning is social before it is individual.

---

## 0:35 – 1:08 ｜ Slide 3 游戏 · 教师视角（2 题）

*（切到游戏页，点 BE THE TEACHER）*

A Grade 8 STEAM class is building a **wind-powered car** that must travel five metres. Let's start as the teacher.

**Scene one.** Half the class is stuck; a student asks what to try next. Do I hand out a checklist? No — I ask the class *why* a wind car might not move, then each group tests its own idea. That's **scaffolding in the zone of proximal development**.

**Scene two.** How do I assess? Not a formula test, not just the distance — but each student's **reflection** on what failed and what they'd change. **Assessment for learning.**

---

## 1:08 – 1:45 ｜ 总结页 → 换角色 → 学生视角（2 题）

*（教师 2 题答完，出现总结页：点 **Play Again · switch role** → 点 **BE THE STUDENT**）*

Now switch sides — same room, student's eyes.

**Scene three.** Four designs, none moves. Do I wait for the teacher? No — I turn to my group: *let's test one thing at a time. Why do the wheels keep spinning?* That's **peer dialogue**: we build the knowledge together.

**Scene four.** Our car went 3.2 metres — not the farthest. What counts? Not the grade, but explaining **why our first four designs failed**. Adams calls that a **learning orientation**.

---

## 1:45 – 1:58 ｜ Slide 4 The Learner

*（切页）*

So the **learner is an active constructor** — arriving with prior knowledge and personal theories, learning by negotiating meaning with others.

---

## 1:58 – 2:10 ｜ Slide 5 The Teacher

The **teacher is a guide**, not the source of truth: authentic tasks, open questions, work inside the ZPD. Brooks and Brooks call it a **paradigm shift** — from transmitting to supporting construction.

---

## 2:10 – 2:28 ｜ Slide 6 Environment & Close

*（切页，放慢）*

And the **environment** is the third teacher: a social space with real tasks, peer talk, and assessment **embedded** in learning.

So — the learner **builds**, the teacher **scaffolds**, the environment **connects**. Thank you.
